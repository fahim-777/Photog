About

I shoot to remember what it felt like.

Based on both coasts, I've spent the last decade on sidelines, in locker rooms and along finish lines — making pictures for the teams, brands and publications building the next era of sport.

My work sits between hard editorial and considered portraiture: fast when it has to be, patient when it counts. I travel light, I work clean, and I send files the same night.
